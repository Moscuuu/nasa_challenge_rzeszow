[https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/) - **NASA**'s primary portal for discovering and accessing Earth science observations.

[https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/index.html](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/index.html) - An easy-to-use interactive environment for visually exploring **NASA** satellite imagery and environmental observations.

[https://pds.nasa.gov/](https://pds.nasa.gov/) - **NASA**'s long-term archive for planetary science data from **NASA** orbital, lander, and rover missions. It provides access to lunar and Martian mission observations spanning imagery, geoscience, atmospheres, plasma environments, cartography, and other measurements.

[https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/) - **LRO** provides extensive observations of the lunar surface through instruments including **LROC**, **LOLA**, Diviner, **LEND**, Mini-RF and others.

[https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm) - Diviner maps lunar surface temperatures and provides especially useful products for studying the lunar poles. Its polar and resource products could be compared with extreme cold, ice-bearing, or other terrestrial environments.

[https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/rcm](https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/rcm) - **RCM** is Canada's new generation of Earth observation satellites. The **RCM** includes a trio of Earth observation satellites, capable of scanning Earth day or night and in any weather conditions. The **RCM** is designed to provide effective solutions in three main areas: Maritime surveillance (ice, surface wind, oil pollution and ship monitoring); Disaster management (mitigation, warning, response and recovery); and Ecosystem monitoring (agriculture, wetlands, forestry and coastal change monitoring).

[https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/radarsat1](https://donnees-data.asc-csa.gc.ca/en/dataset/radarsat1) - Launched in November **1995**, **RADARSAT**-1 provided Canada and the world with an operational radar satellite system capable of timely delivery of large amounts of data. Equipped with a powerful synthetic aperture radar (**SAR**) instrument, it acquired images of the Earth day or night, in all weather and through cloud cover, smoke and haze. All data products in the Canadian **RADARSAT**-1 archive have been released under an open license.

[https://github.com/asc-csa/Synthetic_Aperture_Radar_Tutorial](https://github.com/asc-csa/Synthetic_Aperture_Radar_Tutorial) - his tutorial demonstrates how to work with **SAR** imagery using Python and **QGIS**. It includes easy and practical steps for accessing and visualizing data.

[https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/) - Provides free and open access to Sentinel Earth-observation data covering land, oceans, and atmosphere, with tools for visualization and processing.

[https://opensciencedata.esa.int/](https://opensciencedata.esa.int/) - A catalog of publicly available geoscience datasets, products, experiments, and workflows.

[https://gportal.jaxa.jp/gpr/index/](https://gportal.jaxa.jp/gpr/index/) - Provides Earth-observation products from **JAXA** missions, searchable by variables such as precipitation, snow, vegetation, etc.
